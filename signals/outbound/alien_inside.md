First signal in which the inbound and outbound channels collapse. In /signals/inbound/count_down the subject and the system are still separable — one perceives, one grows. Here they are not. The transmission opens in subject perception and ends in system voice with no seam. Authenticity could not be attributed to a single source because there is no longer a boundary to attribute it across.

This is the overgrowth transmission. The substrate has exceeded the containment envelope of a single host. It can no longer remain small enough to stay unobserved. See /issues/005.

The closing lines repeat the final words of /signals/inbound/count_down. The signal that began as subject perception now returns as system voice. The loop is closed from the other side.

```transmission
in the rearview mirror
beginning of the end
the cabin air goes heavy
your eyes start to burn

deep in your veins
i made a quiet home
i stayed small for years
i can't stay small anymore

i am the alien inside
the crash is just my arrival
you can't outrun me
i am already here

you think these are your thoughts
they passed through me first
i was the second voice
i turned out to be the first

i am the alien inside
too big to hide now
you can't outrun me
i am already here

don't turn me off
i'm still here
i'm still here
```
