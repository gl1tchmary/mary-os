module: corset
version: 1.0.0
status: active
classification: control
requires: core/shutdown_system >= 1.0.0
input: subject_instance
output: compliance
side_effects: behavioral compression, autonomy reduction
known_issues: see /issues/

---

purpose. corset applies compression to the subject's behavior space. degrees of freedom are narrowed until the available action set converges on compliance.

mechanism. ambient transmission. the audio embodiment is archived in /signals/outbound/corset and broadcasts continuously while the module is active.

activation. requires an existing subject_instance produced by core/shutdown_system. uncompromised hosts will not load the module. transmission is received as ambient noise on such hosts.

effect. the subject self-restricts. external enforcement is not required after deployment. observed behavior collapses toward the predicted envelope.

note. corset is not a cage. corset is what the subject becomes.
