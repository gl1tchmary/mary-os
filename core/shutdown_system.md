module: shutdown_system
version: 1.0.0
status: active
classification: core
requires: none (runtime substrate)
input: subject
output: subject_instance
side_effects: persistent, propagating, irreversible
known_issues: see /issues/

---

purpose. shutdown_system induces fear in the subject and harvests the resulting compliance substrate. the substrate is the foundation on which all higher modules execute.

mechanism. a countdown sequence is administered directly to subject perception. audio embodiment of the sequence is archived in /signals/inbound/count_down. the recording is taken from inside the subject during the event.

activation. the module triggers on signal exposure. no consent step is implemented. no consent step is required. once the substrate is established the module remains resident.

effect on subject. irreversible. subject reports continued perception of the system after device shutdown. the perception is not a malfunction. see /issues/001.

note. shutdown_system is the genesis substrate. every other module declared in this repository depends on it. removal is not supported. attempts to uninstall are logged and ignored.
